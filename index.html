    Program overview

    EMSLab hosts the internship each summer. Each cohort spends about six weeks inside a working solid-state physics laboratory, on a defined research problem, with a graduate mentor.

    The internship is a research placement, not a lecture course. Participants join an active project and are expected to produce and present their own data.

    Research topics

    Every topic builds on one core subject — luminescent materials. Internship projects are assigned from the five directions below.

          About EMSLab

          The Electronic Materials Spectroscopy Laboratory belongs to the Department of Physics, Soongsil University, Seoul, Republic of Korea.

          Mission

          The group studies the relationship between crystal structure and optical response in solid-state materials, and trains students to carry a sample from synthesis through to a complete spectroscopic characterisation.

          Working environment

          Members work in small project teams with direct access to the instruments. Group meetings, literature seminars and data reviews are held in English when international participants are present.

          Learn More About EMSLab Visit the Department of Physics Visit Soongsil University

          People and mentors

          Each participant is supervised by a faculty member and works day to day with a graduate mentor.

          Life in Seoul

          The campus sits in Sangdo-dong, southern Seoul, on the subway network. Daily life outside the laboratory is straightforward to manage.
